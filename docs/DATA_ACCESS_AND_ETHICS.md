# Source access, attribution and ethical boundaries

This register distinguishes access from permission to redistribute. Raw and derived datasets, item labels, author identifiers and model weights remain local under the project's established no-data-sharing policy. That policy is not attributed to a clause in the supplied five-page assignment. The public repository contains source code, protocols and aggregate notebook results, not a licensed redistribution of a news corpus.

| Material | Access and attribution | Boundary applied here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Michigan survey releases and historical series | University of Michigan Surveys of Consumers; official pages and release calendar in `sources.md` | Cite the provider and preserve retrieved versions. The project has no respondent-level survey files or exposure records; public aggregate access is not a grant to republish restricted microdata. |
| BLS, BEA, FRED/ALFRED | Official public releases, with series IDs, release dates, retrieval addresses and hashes | Preserve original agency attribution and series-specific source notes. FRED is a distributor; source rights and definitions remain relevant. No synthetic release dates or missing observations are invented. |
| News/RSS/search records | AP, Reuters, NPR, Gothamist, Texas Tribune and Colorado Sun; separately CNBC and Fox brands | Analyze captured title-level material. No access-control bypass, paywall circumvention or article-body scraping is required. Publicly retrievable headlines are not asserted to be openly licensed. Item text stays local; publish aggregate measures and method limitations. |
| Hacker News | Public Algolia search endpoints; titles, dates and retrieval-vintage counts | User names are hashed in analytical records. No identity profiles or individual beliefs are inferred. Votes are not impressions or historical exposure. Unknown bots/reposts remain a limitation. |
| CardiffNLP sentiment model | Pinned `twitter-roberta-base-sentiment-latest` model card and assets; metadata identifies CC-BY-4.0 | Credit CardiffNLP and the linked model research. Weights stay local and hash-checked. Its training domain and release date limit transfer and early historical use. |
| VADER | Hutto and Gilbert; installed distribution's MIT license | Preserve source attribution and package version. Dictionary agreement is not a human validation target. |
| Financial PhraseBank | Malo, Sinha, Takala, Korhonen and Wallenius; pinned authors' distribution | Bundled license is CC-BY-NC-SA-3.0. This is an academic aggregate evaluation. Sentence text, human labels and predictions stay local; original license and README are retained. No commercial dataset release is authorized or implied. |
| World Bank WDI | SP.DYN.TFRT.IN and SP.DYN.CBRT.IN, including original indicator source notes | Preserve entity definitions, data update date, response hashes and attribution. Historical estimates can be revised. Economic income groups are not intelligence groups. No country IQ scores are constructed. |
| Published research statistics | Original papers linked in `READER_MANIFESTO_EVIDENCE.md` | Original chart of attributed numerical summaries, not copied publisher figures or a new participant-level analysis. Evidence, inference and manifesto commitments are separated. |
| ETF market observations | Yahoo adjusted-close responses; instrument definitions from fund issuers | Historical simulations using provider-adjusted return proxies; no trades, account access or future return claims. Licensing/access must be reconsidered before any redistribution or commercial product. |

Source links: [Michigan](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/), [FRED/ALFRED source notes](https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/), [Cardiff model card](https://huggingface.co/cardiffnlp/twitter-roberta-base-sentiment-latest), [VADER license](https://github.com/cjhutto/vaderSentiment/blob/master/LICENSE.txt), [PhraseBank author distribution](https://huggingface.co/datasets/takala/financial_phrasebank), [World Bank data terms](https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/legal/terms-of-use-for-datasets).

No humans were recruited for annotation or experiments. The two-human target-corpus requirement remains unmet. Future recruitment requires the applicable institutional process, consent, privacy protection and a prospectively registered analysis. A proposed experiment and its power calculation are not completed participant research. The manifesto does not infer individual intelligence from poverty, nationality or family size, or assert a measured global genetic decline.
