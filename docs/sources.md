## Michigan methodology and interpretation

- **April 5, 2024 methodological announcement.** Web shares were 25% in April, 50% in May, 75% in June and 100% from July. Method effects can affect changes during the transition. High phone/web correlations do not imply identical levels; Current Conditions was lower on web. Use a ramp/mode sensitivity, exclude transition months as a sensitivity, and discuss the limits of a historical model spanning modes. Excluding April–June alone does not remove a permanent level difference. [Announcement](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/files/methodtransitionannouncement2024.pdf), [technical report](https://data.sca.isr.umich.edu/fetchdoc.php?docid=75437).
- **July 10, 2026 report, revised Sep 4.** Michigan compares official and experimental web histories and concludes the May 2026 low persists under a consistent mode comparison. It reports similar partisan ordering across modes; the decline in Republican self-identification began after the presidential transition, rather than with the web transition. Do not assert that a mode change or party composition mechanically explains all pessimism. [Current revised report](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/files/partisaneconomy202607_revised.pdf).
- **May 22, 2026 report.** National sentiment and subindex trends align with independents despite large partisan level differences. Treat affiliation as a possible confounder/moderator; national weakness cannot simply be assigned to one party's answers. [Report](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/files/independents202605.pdf).
- **April 11, 2025 partisan report.** Party differences predate the current administration and are observed over multiple administrations. Political attribution in text should not itself be coded as factual error or bias. [Report](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/files/partisaneconomy202504.pdf).
- **May 3, 2024 information-source report.** Direct household experience, discussion and media are competing information channels. Corpus supply and engagement cannot establish respondents' actual exposure. [Report](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/files/newssources2024.pdf).
- **October 9, 2026 gasoline/spending report.** Official survey evidence documents cost-of-living and gasoline pressure, motivating household-pressure alternatives to headline macro models. It is contextual evidence, not an estimate that the current corpus caused national sentiment changes. [Report](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/files/consumptionresponse202610.pdf).

## Investment extension: October 17, 2026

- **State Street, SPY issuer page.** SPY seeks exposure to the price and yield performance of the S&P 500 and represents US large-cap equities. Issuer definitions identify the instrument; the backtest uses acquired daily market observations, not today's displayed performance table or expense ratio. [Official SPY page](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/individual/etfs/state-street-spdr-sp-500-etf-trust-spy).
- **State Street, BIL issuer page.** BIL tracks short US Treasury bills with one-to-three-month remaining maturities. The 60% SPY / 40% BIL benchmark is a stock/Treasury-bill allocation, not a broad stock/bond portfolio. BIL is an ETF, not a guaranteed cash deposit. [Official BIL page](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/individual/etfs/state-street-spdr-bloomberg-1-3-month-t-bill-etf-bil).
- **Yahoo Finance, adjusted-close definition and chart data.** Adjusted closes incorporate applicable splits and distributions; adding dividends separately would double count them. The public chart responses actually acquired for SPY and BIL contain daily close, adjusted close, timestamps and corporate-action events. Exact request parameters, saved payloads, hashes and retrieval times are retained in `data/raw/investing/` and `outputs/tables/investment_acquisition.json`. Requests cover January 2009 through October 6, 2026 for signal warm-up and endpoint validation; strategy returns end October 2. These are retrospective provider-adjusted total-return proxies, not executable adjusted-price quotes or an assertion of an officially supported free API. [Yahoo adjustment definition](https://in.help.yahoo.com/kb/adjusted-close-sln28256.html), [SPY chart endpoint](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/SPY), [BIL chart endpoint](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/BIL).
- **FRED / Claudia Sahm, Real-time Sahm Rule Recession Indicator.** The published indicator compares the latest three-month U-3 mean with the minimum of the previous twelve months' means, using information available at that time. This motivates the 0.50-point deterioration threshold; the project's explicitly custom guard uses its latest twelve means including the current mean and actual ALFRED origin snapshots. It is Sahm-style, not an exact reproduction of SAHMREALTIME, and does not establish a recession declaration or investment profitability. [Published definition](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/SAHMREALTIME).
- **SEC Investor.gov, Investor Bulletin: Performance Claims**, September 15, 2022. The bulletin distinguishes hypothetical backtesting from actual investment performance and cautions against treating historical results as a forecast. This supports the extension's separation of positive historical net returns, benchmark outperformance and alpha. [Official bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-47).

The [fixed investment protocol](INVESTMENT_PROTOCOL.md) was recorded before strategy-performance calculation; the [results and audit](INVESTMENT_RESULTS.md) report its failure against the fixed SPY/BIL mix and explain the largely constant exposure shifts in the exploratory overlays. Market acquisition is dated October 17 in New York (October 18 UTC), while the evaluated holding horizon remains January 2, 2015 through October 2, 2026. Current issuer-page observations do not enter the original October 13 economic information set.

## Update protocol

At each refresh, store the release timestamp separately from the observation month, estimate status and retrieval timestamp. Freeze previous vintages. October 23 changes the Michigan target vintage; October 29 adds September PCE and the first Q3 GDP estimate; both are after this project's initial cutoff. November 6 is after election day and belongs to a separately labeled follow-up. Recompute features only from information available at each origin and never backfill those future releases into October 13 predictions.
