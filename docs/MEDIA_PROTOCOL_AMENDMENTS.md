# Media protocol audit

The dimension codebook was written before the first project neural scoring pass. During candidate inspection, the geographic exclusion dictionary was expanded to cover countries/demonyms omitted initially (including Irish, Spanish and Hong Kong). No sentiment target or forecast outcomes were used to make this correction. The final panel has 4,069 titles rather than the temporary first pass's 4,161. Neural title scores are unchanged for identical text and immutable model revision; only new sampled titles were rescored. The independent annotation sample was set to exactly 300 and all temporary blank worksheets were replaced before release to annotators.

RSS publication hours frequently cluster at07:00/08:00UTC, consistent with date-only publisher indexing. They are retained as supplied but never treated as reliable intraday timestamps. The conservative availability proxy is next New York midnight after the feed's publication date. HN creation timestamps are exact provider timestamps; original unedited title versions remain unavailable. The headline retrieval-vintage and historical Google selection limitations remain in all modeling interpretations.

After the frozen panel was scored, an explicitly purposive numeric-alignment diagnostic selected eight same-release-day US unemployment-title percentages across seven BLS releases. The primary release archives were read through web browsing after direct HTTP access returned403. Values, release dates, official observation periods, URLs and browser evidence are preserved. Each title's percentage matches the official release value. This is a limited numeric calibration check and not a random factual-accuracy audit: definitions and often observation periods are absent from headlines, RSS intraday precision is uncertain, and all other claims remain unverified. `factual_error_rate` therefore remains missing and `verified_claim_count` remains zero. Additional numeric alignment columns are counts of checked/matching examples, never an accuracy claim.

Inspection of the original300 annotation pool identified clear word-sense errors: cosmic/balloon inflation, the store Economy Candy, the entertainment segment Not My Job, Steve Jobs as a proper name, and unrelated individual political/sport/court employment references. A deterministic post-sampling relevance screen removes these and additional explicit foreign titles; it retains ambiguous household stories, Georgia (also a US state), Jordan (also a personal name), and Puerto Rico (a US territory). No removed title is replaced by a new sampled title. Final corpus:3,787 titles;282 removals from the4,069 pre-screen draw. The original300 independent annotation texts, source key and frozen neural/dictionary outputs are retained; eligibility flags distinguish the final-corpus subset. This refinement uses document inspection and therefore turns annotation performance into development diagnostic evidence, not an untouched holdout estimate. Forecast targets and outcomes were not used to choose exclusion rules. The codebook dimensions, sentiment checkpoint and scoring thresholds were not fitted to these judgments.

The model acquisition path now works without sibling assignments: a sibling immutable cache is optional, and a fresh installation downloads the official fixed HuggingFace revision, verifies official LFS SHA-256 values where supplied, and records local response provenance/hashes for every asset. The fresh-download path and tamper rejection are tested with deterministic mocked official responses, avoiding a redundant half-gigabyte download. Scoring remains offline after acquisition.
